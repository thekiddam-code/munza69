# Offline Earth lunar presentation

`web/vendor/astronomy.js` is the unchanged, readable browser/Node distribution of **Astronomy Engine 2.1.19** by Don Cross, MIT license included in its header. Source: https://github.com/cosinekitty/astronomy ; pinned npm archive: https://registry.npmjs.org/astronomy-engine/-/astronomy-engine-2.1.19.tgz . No runtime package installation, network lookup or external command is used.

`lunar.js` calls the library's geocentric MoonPhase and SearchMoonPhase(180°), which include the library's dynamical time/UTC conversion. ICARUS declares UTC years **2000–2100** supported. Outside that interval it returns an explicit unsupported result rather than an approximate full moon. Full-moon atmosphere applies on the **configured timezone's local date containing the calculated full-moon instant**, not a fixed mean-cycle window. Two full moons in one month are supported. Calculation caches at most 24 UTC months; it never changes physical simulation state or pacing.

Independent verification: NASA/GSFC *Phases of the Moon: 2001 to 2100*, Universal Time, https://eclipse.gsfc.nasa.gov/phase/phases2001.html , retrieved 2026-11-10. `test_lunar.js` contains 16 published times (three 2024 dates and all thirteen 2026 full moons). Each must agree within two minutes of the rounded published time; this is a sampled validation tolerance, not a claim of universal two-minute astronomical accuracy. Timezone and May 2026 double-full-moon tests are separate. USNO's API returned HTTP 503 during investigation; no USNO verification is claimed.

Personal observances remain explicitly configured and opt-in: annual MM-DD or one-time YYYY-MM-DD, optional inclusive YYYY-MM-DD `until` (at most 366 days after start) and `enabled:false`. Annual February 29 occurs only in leap years. No religious-calendar conversion or demographic inference. Acknowledgments require a free, healthy, stationary individual at an existing meal workstation with actual paid food carried; observances do not create or charge a second meal. Legacy explicitly configured MM-DD entries continue unchanged.
