# v1.4 limitations and next core work

This is an anthology recalibration, not a finished commercial release. Read FINAL_DESIGN.md and FINAL_RELEASE_ROADMAP.md. Behavioral tests cannot establish whether every story is engaging, every interface polished or every crew response convincing.

- The external interaction vocabulary is small and uses state-conditioned templates, not unrestricted dialogue or a narrative model. The same kind of signal can recur. Philosophical exchanges preserve questions rather than simulating genuine semantic debate. Deepen specificity/character responses and human review before adding more systems.
- Visitors are modeled through local communications and persistent registry/history, not physical passenger boarding, new walkable interiors or recruitment. Some signals need no actor. Returning channels and links are supported, but appearance is not guaranteed. Registry materialization remains capped at 256; existing records are never deleted to meet a quota.
- Core has five finite local venues plus ship context. It deliberately does not keep creating planets or destinations to supply missions. Healthy closed-cycle operation can stop travel/extraction for long periods. Finite supply failure is possible; resupply is not guaranteed forever.
- Existing crew social behavior can generate many source records. New arc formation is throttled and more selective, but numerical relationship/experience models and generic liaison text still need authored polish. Traditions require actual participants and paid provisions; they are not full new interactive ceremonies. Not every raw event becomes a story, and unresolved research endings do not add fabricated scientific results.
- Research/custody/Lazarus rules are preserved. A narrative research thread with an explicit case ID can wait for or summarize subsequent actual experiments; it does not itself conduct an experiment or certify replication. Broad field-observation threads without a case ID retain uncertainty rather than guessing a case match; automatic finding-to-case narrative linkage needs refinement. No guaranteed Lazarus breakthrough, resurrection or central mystery ending exists.
- Field expeditions and rescue retain v1.3 limits: two to four crew, shared primary pod, local one-person EVA capability, finite supplies, range/availability gates and no free replacement of an unrecovered pod. Large-scale rescue or towing is not added.
- Operations is more cohesive, but existing specialist pages and old labels elsewhere still need consolidation. Preference changes need a subsequent save. Frequency controls optional new signal/visitor-channel opportunities; disabling them does not disable native crew/ship/research stories, ongoing exchanges or incidental contacts physically encountered during local operations. The tablet remains a telemetry/controller subset; it does not expose full authoring, preferences, history import/export or chronicle playback.
- Sunday presentation is sourced cards/replays with category-based tone, not an authored film edit, audio score or emotional-understanding system. A selected tone summarizes recorded counts and can understate nuance in a mixed week. No new camera footage, violence or lethal highlight is generated.
- Significant history remains retained, so storage grows with actual activity. Three transactional snapshots plus indexed history and compressed cache do not provide infinite storage or instant startup. Large export/import still allocates browser memory. Exported backups protect against origin/profile deletion; no cloud backup exists. Multi-tab writer arbitration is not certified.
- Explicit import preserves legacy galaxy records, so a large migrated v1.3 save remains large even though new core scans are frozen. Core and v1.3 use independent writable database/cache keys. Notes and UI preferences can remain shared. Downgrading a v1.4 backup to an older executable is unsupported.

No physical Windows/Android/TV, Firefox/Safari, real LAN-disconnection, audio accessibility or hostile multi-tab certification is claimed. Automated tests use Linux Chromium, desktop/tablet viewports, real IndexedDB and a real loopback authenticated bridge with desktop input stubbed. Synthetic adverse fixtures are labeled separately from normal multi-seed voyages. Passing normal seeds does not guarantee every seed survives or every rare outcome occurs.

The next batch should deepen existing interaction specificity and character responses, consolidate interface navigation, improve sourced weekly selection/SOL explanations, stream large saves and establish single-writer ownership. Then run broader seeded/hardware tests and sustained human play review. Procedural galaxy expansion, territory/factions and large-scale markets remain in the preserved Extended Universe edition; they are not the core roadmap.
