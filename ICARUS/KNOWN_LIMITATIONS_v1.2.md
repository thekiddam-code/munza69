# v1.2 limits and recommended next batch

This is a complete local frontier loop with abstract travel and field operations, not an astronomical simulator. Coordinates, fuel and chemistry use documented normalized units. Sector generation can be extended indefinitely, but saves retain explored identity/evidence and therefore grow with exploration. One-year storage budgets do not establish unlimited-duration browser storage safety. Export backups regularly; IndexedDB archival is the recommended next storage step.

Teams remain two people with one shared transport. New planets/moons are three-transect field abstracts rather than fully navigable surface maps. Orbital operations use the shared exploration/return lifecycle, without orbital mechanics, a multi-hop fuel optimizer or a separate route waypoint editor. Captain hold applies at orbit; an in-transit ship continues its paid leg. A compound failure or insufficient reachable fuel can strand the ship; the universe does not manufacture rescue deposits.

Dry cargo capacity excludes habitation supplies, installed equipment and compact samples. No bulk cargo jettison/trading UI or station economy is included. Astro-mining, ice harvesting and salvage use the common extraction mechanism and different finite destination deposits. Distress signals currently yield telemetry evidence rather than social NPC encounters or rescued crews. Environmental change is persisted daily hazard/weather and real equipment damage, not a fully simulated ecosystem/faction economy.

Scientific interest rankings and evidence-driven field opportunities coexist with the existing hypothesis/experiment system. This is not open-ended language-model scientific reasoning. Conclusions are scoped composition evidence; Lazarus has no guaranteed breakthrough. No new combat/kill-cam generator is implemented. Existing rare documented eligibility remains, and footage/reconstructions retain their established labels.

Lifetime provenance aggregates preserve all new transaction amounts by resource/source; recent individual transaction views are bounded. Older v1.1 history cannot be reconstructed retroactively. Resource audits prove normalized inventory consistency, not physical chemistry. Model source-backed solar power is not calibrated to a real stellar luminosity/distance model.

The legacy HTML remains large; frontier and storage code was isolated into modules, while wholesale UI/engine migration was deferred to protect established behavior. Compression is synchronous; very large saves can pause rendering. lz-string is pinned locally with its MIT license. Portable exports are ordinary JSON; older binaries cannot directly read newly compressed browser slots.

Automated UI coverage uses Linux Chromium at desktop/tablet viewport sizes and a loopback paired bridge. Physical Windows keyboard/mouse controls, actual Android hardware, Samsung TV integration, Safari/Firefox, and real LAN/network interruptions were not tested in this batch. Automated adverse fixtures are labeled and are not natural campaign events. Two acceptance seeds do not establish balance/survival for all seeds or every compound emergency.

Recommended next batch: IndexedDB append-only historical storage and provenance viewer; waypoint/fuel-feasibility planning with rescue logistics; richer surfaces and equipment-specific extraction; configurable team size/multiple transport; persistent third-party contacts with evidence-backed distress/rescue; broader seed fuzzing, real-device accessibility tests, and extraction balance calibration using the packaged metrics.
