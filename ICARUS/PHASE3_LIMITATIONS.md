# Phase 3 remaining limitations

- This is a functional development increment, not a declaration of final artwork, complete character intelligence or human-approved story quality. Final release still needs sustained human playtesting.
- Characters use deterministic numeric preferences, existing traits, physical constraints and bounded memories. Full inherited-controller belief reasoning, rich psychological interpretation and autonomous recovery of dead carriers' records remain Phase 2 limitations. Creator/debug access and backups can inspect private state; privacy is an in-game knowledge rule, not multi-user encryption.
- Procedural silhouettes, contextual arm/prop cues and schematic snapshot occupancy are not final anatomical/hand animation, rendered film or cinematic production. The Sunday player has text and schematic state frames, no voice synthesis, soundtrack, video encoding or exported film. Rich authored shot composition and accessibility/human pacing review remain open.
- Captures are sparse, only working onboard cameras, last 120 changed frames; films retain 12 weeks. Most source highlights have no precisely matching frame and are honestly reconstructed as event cards. No old save gains fictional prior footage. A sparse quiet archive is shorter than the 3–5-minute target. No guaranteed awards, extraordinary kills or dramatic endings. Legendary positive-path tests are explicitly constructed fixtures, not naturally observed campaign claims.
- SOL uses finite source-backed text templates with persistent references, not a language-model service or unsourced beliefs. Its bounded watch-note layer supplements existing operational commentary; broader editorial consolidation/repetition review remains necessary.
- Full actual history/knowledge still grow. Presentation memories/snapshots/films are bounded, but multi-year archival streaming, single-writer coordination and very large backups remain open. Thirty-real-day measurements do not prove unbounded-duration reliability.
- Linux Chromium tests and tablet/mobile emulation do not establish physical Windows launcher/input, Galaxy Tab S5e GPU/touch, Android LAN, Samsung TV, Firefox or Safari behavior. Audio, final art and human long-form review cannot be certified by automated tests. Native hardware and human review remain release gates. No antivirus engine or penetration test is claimed.
- The inherited trusted-LAN HTTP/control and TV certificate/dependency limitations remain; see SECURITY_PHASE3.md. Core/Extended Universe separation is preserved; no planetary/faction/economic expansion was added.
