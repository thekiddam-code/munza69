# Phase 3 architecture and engineering changes

The Phase 2 simulation, paid resources/repair, physical graph, knowledge ledger, source history, ecology and wall calendar remain authoritative. No new universe or replacement simulation was introduced. The host, launcher, port, save namespace and Android thin-client authority are unchanged.

- `crew-life.js`: additive seeded individual preferences and counters, bounded last 12 routine choices and 24 actual interaction memories/person. Existing emergency/maintenance/research/rest priorities precede ordinary reachable task selection. Conversations require fit, available co-located actors and a legitimate information/social need; actual sharing uses `knowledge.speak`. Conversations occupy two minutes. Support and report-exchange discussion stays private; a company conversation is acknowledged publicly only when both persistent sociability values exceed .65. Conversation records cannot recursively become gossip topics. No invented personal history, arguments or romance.
- `cinema.js`: pure contextual spectator selection; recording only at physical ticks, at most every 15 simulation minutes, camera power/receiver and actual occupancy required. Last 120 changed snapshots retained. Each stores real actor identity/node/body/pose, room condition and coincident source references. No room footage for unobserved field/corridor actors. `cinema-ui.js` is optional local observer direction. Existing manual camera remains available. Actor markup caching avoids rebuilding unchanged sprites. Historical review now uses recorded actors, routes, poses and conditions instead of substituting current crew.
- `sol-voice.js`: bounded 48 commentary entries, 192 source receipts and expiring repeat signatures. Candidates are legitimately delivered knowledge or existing public operational telemetry, preserving source/confidence. Quiet and emergency-safe templates; memory references only actual saved entries, not inferred beliefs. No recursive story events or external AI/network dependency. Existing SOL operational authority is unchanged.
- `chronicle-film.js`: pure source-filtered weekly compilation; physical Monday boundary archives once, with 12 bounded films. Public highlights, recorded participants, saved unresolved threads, sourced SOL assessment and credits; no filler for quiet weeks. Actual matched snapshots are copied into films before rolling-buffer expiry; missing imagery stays explicitly RECONSTRUCTION. Live UI preview never creates recordings or archives. `film-ui.js` provides a nonmodal play/pause/previous/skip timeline independent of world time, with schematic captured occupancy and source labels. Target length is shorter when the recorded week cannot support 3–5 minutes.
- Legendary selection requires existing exceptional-lethal criteria, a retained source-linked snapshot and corroborating public death record. A recording ID without its content is insufficient. No exceptional flag, recording or death is synthesized to fill this segment.

`world-schema.js` validates optional additive groups. Older saves omit them; bootstrap initializes empty memories/recordings/commentary, never fictional historical footage. Export identifies 1.7.0-phase3 while existing save format/version/namespace remain compatible. Cinematic/crew audits and counts extend daily diagnostics without removing existing fields.

Tradeoffs: the schematic player is intentionally in-app rather than a video encoder. Captures are sparse and bounded, so reconstruction cards are usual and old weeks may have no imagery. Numeric preferences/templates are inspectable deterministic models, not human-level character reasoning. Full source history remains persistent and growing; bounded presentation buffers do not solve archival streaming or concurrent writers. See PHASE3_LIMITATIONS.md and measured PHASE3_VALIDATION.md.
