# Limits and release gates

This is a functional integrated Living Personalities build, not a final-art or physical-device sign-off. Test results are PHASE42B_VALIDATION.md and the delivery reports.

- No adulthood/orientation is inferred. Old voyages lacking explicit adult metadata will develop ordinary relationships but cannot generate mature private events. Creator can confirm authored adulthood; it cannot pick a partner or grant willingness. Existing named restrictions survive migration. Do not describe a technical confirmed-adult fixture as the user's organic voyage.
- Personality/romance behavior is a bounded simulation model, not human-level intent or a guarantee of couples. Invitations can be declined and repeated attempts are limited. Broad authored preference/orientation import and richer boundary histories remain future refinement; no orientation label is invented.
- Presentation is original procedural non-explicit art. Off/Romance/Mature-Implied and fade-to-black work; dedicated kissing, handholding, seated/shared-blanket/dressing and optional non-sexual nudity assets are not complete. Some affectionate cues reuse gestures. No explicit art exists.
- Existing room/station graph is physically authoritative. Fine furniture collision, crowd avoidance, full door-leaf occlusion and guaranteed no sprite overlap remain incomplete. Distances are abstract travel minutes; no measured metre/second claim. Corridor cameras show one station rather than compressing the entire deck. Ladder coverage remains labeled reconstruction, not installed video.
- The one shared lift supports queued capacity-four same-route batches, power/service stalls and persistent passengers. Multi-stop dispatch optimization and a full incapacitated-passenger cabin rescue workflow are not complete. A blocked cabin can require actual operational recovery; no automatic medical teleport or survival guarantee was introduced.
- Quarters are shared existing rooms. Private events require an unoccupied safe room and can wait, decline or fail; no extra quarters were generated. Lighting/doors retain the existing atmosphere model, not a new airtight-door fluid simulation. Private occupancy/intent is not disclosed by ordinary cameras.
- Classified access is local-host-only in this first foundation. Ordinary paired phones cannot unlock it. No remote classified role provisioning, secret mission system, surveillance-consequence arc or covert video recording. Field-of-view filtering uses the existing scene coordinates and rectangle, not full furniture occlusion. No hidden organism is automatically detected or identified.
- The production pacing selector now supports persistent 20×, explicitly superseding the old phase's deferral. Holidays remain 1×. Existing six-hour suspension catch-up and bounded work remain; long offline gaps are logged rather than an unlimited simulation promise. Measured effective rates apply to the recorded Linux test, not all Windows hardware.
- Existing genuine source history remains retained and grows. Bounded recent memories, habits, private-event summaries and camera state do not prove unbounded-voyage save growth solved.
- No physical Windows, Samsung Android/TV, native APK rebuild, real LAN/Tailscale, antivirus or human accessibility/GPU validation was performed. Native source/APKs and installed Android functionality are preserved. No antivirus was disabled. Use the provided manual procedures before replacing a working installation.

Full production polish and those explicit gaps remain open. Passing tests alone does not complete the ICARUS final design. Next work should deepen these existing connected-ship/relationship/presentation/recovery features, not add universe breadth.
