# Phase 4.6 known limitations

- Gym remains deferred; A03 Observation Lounge and A09 Crew Lounge are preserved.
- Exploration uses the existing25-sector asteroid, not a new giant underground map. Industrial facilities, caverns, passage/lift gates, military seals and hidden borehole canon remain. No new geological universe or automatic dangerous discovery.
- Movement is deterministic graph segments and50-minute local approach, not orbital/rigid-body physics or measured FOV/collision geometry. Drones conservatively respect blocked/unpowered lift paths. Co-located camera coverage uses the existing sector abstraction.
- Two paid chassis maximum; an irretrievable chassis still counts. Replacements beyond that limit, a carrier vehicle, precision mid-passage salvage, independently dispatched rescue craft and dedicated drone-salvage missions outside existing crew expedition transport are deferred. Stationary co-located recovery and returning-team custody work; endpoint teleport recovery is rejected.
- Sensors use available existing environmental and utility/object values. They do not invent thermal maps, detailed spectroscopy, CO2 concentrations, new specimens, Xenomorph sightings or scientific conclusions. Damaged channels return no reading. No realistic optics, radio propagation solver or new battery/propulsion damage physics.
- Event records are short sequences of captured procedural PNGs and source states at simulated-minute cadence, with a three-state prebuffer and up to two following states. They are not encoded audiovisual video or physical camera recordings. Later state rendering is explicitly reconstruction. Camera failure can prevent capturing its own failure; no missing image is fabricated.
- The paired remote receives the latest record and up to three frame states; PNGs may be omitted to honor the existing publish-size limit. Such remote previews are labeled reconstruction. Full bounded captured records remain in the host save. Separate high-resolution on-demand media transfer is deferred.
- Automatic build/launch/repair/recovery depends on actual crew availability, ship needs, received knowledge, resources and safety. No deadline, guaranteed survey, guaranteed return, dramatic encounter or survival guarantee. Optional management directives cannot bypass physical/safety gates.
- Existing relays can extend real connected signal and existing crew controls can deploy equipment. No new dedicated autonomous beacon-installation planner or drone-deployed relay inventory is claimed. Retrieval proposals arise only from actual received stranded reports; unknown lost positions are not supplied omnisciently.
- Large-save streaming/worker parsing and arbitrary-duration full-history retention remain inherited limitations. New working sets are bounded, but real history still grows.
- Windows laptop, Samsung phone/Tab S5e/TV, native APK, actual LAN/Tailscale, hardware GPU/touch/accessibility, human visual/playability review and antivirus testing are not available here. Linux Chromium, local HTTP and emulation are not physical-device certification. Native Android source recovery/build remains pending, not permanently unsupported. Existing native/TV functionality is retained.

Recommended next batch: physical Windows/phone boot and recon review; measured field-camera visibility and movement; source-backed drone retrieval/relay planning and on-demand captured-media transport. Keep gym and broader universe work in their separate backlogs.
