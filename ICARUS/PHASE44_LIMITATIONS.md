# Phase 4.4 limitations and Phase4.5 recommendations

This is a working integrated expedition increment, not a declaration that the entire final game or every possible field capability is finished. See PHASE44_VALIDATION.md for executed evidence.

- Terrain is the persistent existing25-cell graph with timed connected passages and irregular pixel reconstructions. It is not a coordinate collision/occlusion/rigid-body or planetary simulator. Fine camera FOV and per-fixture optical detail remain limited; stylized industrial backgrounds are not certified optical recordings.
- Complete supported team profile is a qualified Engineer + Scientist buddy expedition. Existing manifests support2–4 people, but there is no new specialist team editor, safe solo certification or wheelchair terrain certification. Existing wheelchair ship movement is preserved. No expertise is invented to fill roles.
- Retrieval supports surviving dropped-device/evidence recovery and same-team injury withdrawal. Independent rescue deployment, stranded-team transfer, carried patients/bodies, a second shuttle and rover fleet are **not implemented**. Lost transport remains unavailable; emergencies are not automatically rescued.
- Environmental protection uses paid suits/instruments, finite supplies, condition, modeled reserve/hazard checks and an abstract independent EVA vestibule. No detailed gas chemistry, full suit pressure leakage/thermal physiology or separate suit-workshop overhaul. Legacy suit condition registers a declared baseline, not inferred historical wear.
- Initial plan telemetry uses a fixed650-minute planning estimate, not a condition-calibrated ETA. Actual work/travel uses the modeled durations and may exceed that estimate; better historical ETA/readiness explanations remain refinement work.
- Repair targets modeled utility equipment with paid progress and post-tests. Not every decorative industrial object is operable. The asteroid power network is coarse and local, not a new large electrical simulator. No certified fully autonomous recovery from every catastrophic combination.
- Significant evidence is saved **source-state event reconstruction, not recorded video**. Local ring3, clips12/device, devices32, archive64, event keys128 and history40 bound the new working sets. Some older injury/event types remain in the canonical journal rather than receiving a dedicated field visual trigger. Remote preview retains only3 frames each for the latest4 records; full bounded records remain saved.
- Field radio reuses existing channel/range/relay/interference and paid-kit shuttle link. It is not a detailed terrain RF simulator. Last-received maps may be stale. No fixed asteroid camera is invented where none is installed.
- Legacy expeditions already underway when loading retain their controller and progress until return; only new asteroid departures use the full physical ops44 sequence. Old legacy biological behavior is not silently rewritten as a save migration. New ops44 passive survey does not energize sealed military controls or activate dormant threats.
- Existing full durable source history still grows with real events; bounded new evidence does not solve arbitrary-duration archive retention. Seven-real-day campaign coverage is not365-day certification. Tested sizes/timings and any failures are reported explicitly.
- Physical Windows laptop, Samsung phone/Tab S5e/TV, installed APK, real LAN/Tailscale, human accessibility/GPU and antivirus validation are unavailable here. Linux Chromium emulation/HTTP testing is not hardware testing. Native Android source recovery/build remains pending; existing functionality is preserved, not declared unsupported.

Recommended Phase4.5: finish independent rescue/casualty logistics with real capacity and source custody; specialist team/readiness UI and explicit repair retry explanations; per-fixture field art/FOV/lighting with human review; broader adverse/long-duration seeds and archive performance. Drone exploration/recovery remains separately approved future scope, not a hidden feature of this build. Preserve chronology, knowledge and core anthology boundaries before expanding scope.
