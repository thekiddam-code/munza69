# Phase 2 boundaries and remaining work

This is an implemented Living World increment, not final-release certification. Read PHASE2_VALIDATION.md for measured evidence and FINAL_RELEASE_ROADMAP.md for release gates.

- The new ledger integrates field discoveries/reports/maps, military archives, custody/science intake, creature/clinical observations, conversations, records, briefings and actual NPC contacts. Existing shipwide operational dashboards remain central telemetry. A complete rewrite of every inherited social/anthology controller into individual belief reasoning is not claimed. Current confidence is evidence uncertainty, not a sophisticated model of deliberate lies, belief revision or personality-specific interpretation.
- Private canon, captain actor-perspective views and full backups remain inspectable in this single-player application. They are not encrypted secrets or a multi-user authorization system. Historical published records cannot be made undiscovered retroactively; migration honestly identifies them as legacy records without inventing witnesses.
- Body-record recovery is physically checked in the ledger API. A full autonomous search/recovery expedition planner and dedicated recovery UI are not implemented. Records on an unrecovered deceased carrier remain inaccessible until a legitimate recovery occurs.
- After three consecutive boarding failures, automatic retry waits for a changed actual route-access signature or captain intervention. Failure does not consume a field-survey attempt; unused cargo/suits return, while launch energy stays spent.
- Existing field missions move their party on a shared grid. No independently navigating split-party field simulation or newly merged planet/ship coordinate engine is claimed. Radio checks use the inherited relay model, including its conservative lost/intermittent classifications.
- Creature ecology is deliberately small: existing organisms, local routes/stimuli, finite metabolism, finite real-body feeding, environmental harm and paid consequences. Measured ship atmosphere supplies pressure/fire conditions; field grids do not yet provide a complete pressure model. It is not a food-web simulator, elaborate scent/acoustic engine, weapon/containment-command expansion, parasite-specific treatment system or complex reproductive colony AI. Android hosts remain excluded. Unknown legacy field locations are preserved without guessing; unrecovered transport organisms stay contained. No guarantees of natural releases, attacks, deaths or outbreaks are provided.
- Character poses/build/mobility cues are procedural schematics. Wheelchair/prosthetic cues are initial readable support, not final anatomical artwork or detailed locomotion. Camera art modes remain; holographic/cutaway geometry is schematic, and historical frames lacking nav metadata cannot acquire invented paths. Optional audio and cinematic production remain unfinished.
- Lunar support is declared for UTC years 2000–2100 and sampled against published NASA times, not independently validated over every instant. Outside the range, lunar markers explicitly report unsupported. Personal Gregorian dates are opt-in; automatic religious calendar conversion, inferred beliefs/birthdays and an authored tradition/decorations library are outside this increment.
- Knowledge/history are persistent records and grow with actual observations. Message delivery receipts and creature traces are bounded, but the complete knowledge ledger is not streamed out of the active snapshot. Large backups/history and hostile concurrent browser tabs remain performance/reliability work. Ordinary startup resumes the simulation under existing bounded catch-up policy; the paused UI state remains session-local.
- Physical Windows BAT execution/desktop input, physical Android/Wi-Fi and TV hardware, Firefox/Safari, hostile-network penetration testing and an antivirus engine scan were unavailable. Android touch emulation and stubbed bridge I/O are identified separately in the reports. Host HTTP/TLS and dependency-range limitations are documented in SECURITY_PHASE2.md. No antivirus policy was disabled or bypassed.
- Passing deterministic tests does not establish sustained story interest, full character individuality or final visual polish. Human playtesting and broader long-duration seed/compound-failure coverage remain necessary. Core ICARUS still excludes galactic procedural/faction/economic expansion.
